# Model Assumptions

The dashboard compares two rental properties over a user-defined holding period.

## Financing assumptions

- Purchase price
- Down payment
- Mortgage interest rate
- Mortgage term
- Holding period

## Operating assumptions

- Monthly rent
- Annual rent growth
- Vacancy rate
- Property taxes
- Insurance
- Property-management costs
- Maintenance and other operating expenses

## Exit assumptions

- Annual property appreciation
- Selling costs
- Remaining mortgage balance at sale

## Interpretation

Results are estimates based on the supplied assumptions. They do not guarantee future investment performance. Users should independently verify financing terms, taxes, insurance, rental demand, repair costs, and property-specific risks.
