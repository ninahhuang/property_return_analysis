# Methodology

## Overview

The model projects each property’s rental income, operating expenses, mortgage balance, property value, equity, sale proceeds, and total return over the selected holding period.

## Annual operating results

For each year, the model calculates scheduled rent, vacancy loss, collected rent, property taxes, insurance, management fees, other operating expenses, net operating income, and annual cash flow.

## Property value and equity

Property value is projected using the selected annual appreciation rate. Ending equity equals projected property value minus the remaining mortgage balance.

## Sale proceeds

Net sale proceeds equal the projected property value, less selling costs and the remaining mortgage balance.

## Total investment result

Total profit includes cumulative operating cash flow and net sale proceeds, less the initial cash investment.

## Sensitivity analysis

The model evaluates how results change under alternative appreciation, rent-growth, and vacancy-rate assumptions.

## Important limitation

The analysis is a scenario-based comparison, not a property valuation, loan quote, tax opinion, or prediction of future returns.
